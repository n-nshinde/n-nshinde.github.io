Recommended reading

Knowledge-Guided Machine Learning: Accelerating Discovery using Scientific Knowledge and Data, edited by Karpatne, Kannan and Kumar (Chapman & Hall/CRC, 2022). A framework for building scientific knowledge into ML models, especially useful with limited data or when moving beyond purely data-driven approaches.
