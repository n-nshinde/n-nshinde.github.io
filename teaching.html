Prospective students & research assistants

I work with students interested in environmental and development economics, especially land and property rights, water, and climate adaptation, using causal inference and spatial data.

Prospective Ph.D. and master's students: the O'Neill School admits Ph.D. and master's students through centralized admissions, so please apply through the O'Neill program you are interested in. I am happy to have an email conversation beforehand about research fit.

Current IU students: if you are interested in research assistant work, please reach out by email. I am also happy to meet during my office hours.
