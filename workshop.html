Social Cost of Water Pollution Workshop

An annual workshop building a research community around quantifying the social cost of water pollution, with regional and temporal estimates.

I have co-organized the annual Social Cost of Water Pollution Workshop since 2023. This initiative is a joint effort by Prof. David Keiser (Yale), Prof. Cathy Kling (Cornell), and Prof. Dan Phaneuf (University of Wisconsin–Madison), in collaboration with the Cornell Atkinson Center for Sustainability and the U.S. Environmental Protection Agency (EPA).

The workshop brings together economists, policy experts, engineers and other professionals to discuss Integrated Assessment Models (IAMs) for water quality, and to foster interdisciplinary collaboration and policy dialogue.

GOAL

Build a research community focused on quantifying the social cost of water pollution.

FOCUS

Integrated Assessment Models for water quality, with regional and temporal estimates.

PARTNERS

Cornell Atkinson Center for Sustainability · U.S. EPA
