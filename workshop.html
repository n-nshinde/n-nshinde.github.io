Community

Research communities I organize and belong to.

Social Cost of Water Pollution Workshop

I have co-organized the annual Social Cost of Water Pollution Workshop since 2023. This initiative is a joint effort by Prof. David Keiser (Yale), Prof. Cathy Kling (Cornell), and Prof. Dan Phaneuf (University of Wisconsin–Madison), in collaboration with the Cornell Atkinson Center for Sustainability and the U.S. Environmental Protection Agency (EPA).

The workshop brings together economists, policy experts, engineers and other professionals to discuss Integrated Assessment Models (IAMs) for water quality, and to foster interdisciplinary collaboration and policy dialogue. For programs and past agendas, visit the Cornell Atkinson Center's workshop page ↗.

Dhar India Studies Program

I am an affiliated faculty member of the Dhar India Studies Program ↗ in IU's Hamilton Lugar School of Global and International Studies. The program offers an undergraduate major and minor in India Studies, a Ph.D. minor, and talks and events on India. My work on groundwater, irrigation and climate shocks in India connects closely with its community.

Center for Latin American and Caribbean Studies (CLACS)

I am also affiliated with CLACS ↗, also in the Hamilton Lugar School. The center offers interdisciplinary training, language instruction and academic events on Latin America and the Caribbean. My research on property rights, land tenure and deforestation in the Brazilian Amazon is part of this community.
